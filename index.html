about me

I'm a Digital Communication and Media student at IPB University with a background in Multimedia. What began as a childhood hobby in writing and storytelling has evolved into a passion I explore across different creative fields.

Through hands-on projects, team collaborations, organizations, and events, I've developed adaptability and a continuous drive to learn. I believe creativity isn't bound to one medium, it's about communicating ideas, telling stories, and creating meaningful work.

VISUAL JOCKEY
Behind the Screen, Bringing the Event to Life

Stepping into the role of a Visual Jockey for MPKMB IPB 63 x AGSN 2026 was an unexpected yet rewarding challenge. As part of the technical production team, I operated the main videotron and managed live visual playback via vMix, from initial rehearsals at Sympora through both event days of MPKMB IPB 63 and AGSN 2026. Mastering technical cues, timing, and coordination under pressure was nerve-racking at first, proving that the best experiences often come from opportunities you never saw coming.
